01 Principles

How this differs from buying another subscription

Buying a tool takes five minutes. The hard part is making it work on your data, under your rules, and still be running three months later.

Integration first

Connected to the CRM, inbox, documents and systems you already use. Not one more separate tab.

Bound to a metric

Before deployment we agree what gets measured: cycle time, quality, cost or conversion. No baseline, no build.

Human in the loop

AI prepares and proposes. A person decides and is accountable. Actions are logged and reversible.

Compliance is not a bolt-on

GDPR basis, data minimisation and a processing record — before the first line of code, not after the audit.

04 Honesty

When a tool is not enough

A product speeds up the work as it is. If the process itself was designed before AI, speed only raises the cost of that process.

A product is enough if

  • → The work repeats and the output is well defined
  • → The process is broadly right, just slow or expensive
  • → Someone will actually use the tool daily

You need Stage 3 if

  • ✕ It is unclear whether this work should exist at all
  • ✕ The constraint is decision quality, not speed
  • ✕ You need a proven financial effect, not faster work

Redesign first. Automate second.

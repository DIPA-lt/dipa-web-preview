01 Qualification

This stage fits if AI is still random

If the team already has a standard and an owner — start at stage 2 or 3. This product is the first stop, not a substitute for the whole transformation.

It fits if

  • → AI is used at random, there is no competence standard
  • → The leader wants to see what good AI competence looks like
  • → There are real tasks that can be measured before and after
  • → You want a change in the work, not an “AI training certificate”

It does not fit if

  • ✕ You want a measured effect number in one process (that is the Sprint)
  • ✕ The team already works to one standard
  • ✕ You want a tool review or a prompt pack

03 How it runs

Real tasks. Not theoretical exercises.

Leaders work with their actual week. If a task cannot be shown in the work — we do not use it.

1

Task

We pick one repeating executive task and lock a baseline.

2

Method

We learn to do it with AI: context, limits, human-in-the-loop.

3

Measure

We repeat the same task. We compare to the baseline — not to a feeling.

4

Standard

We capture the way of working. The leader gets a method to judge others.

Basic AI literacy is a module, not a product. Here you buy a leader's capability and measurement.

04 Measurement
TIME

The same task — faster, without losing quality.

QUALITY

Fewer errors, clearer output, less rewriting.

OUTPUT

More finished work in the same executive time.

REWORK

Fewer returns and fixes after the first cycle.

Saved hours are not a result by themselves. The result is what happened to those hours. If you want a process EBITDA number — that is stage 3.

05 Common questions
Is this the same “AI training” DIPA used to run?
The format is similar — sessions, practice. What you buy is different. Before, literacy was sold. Now — a change in the leader's work and a method to judge competence. If you only need an open workshop, that is an Academy question, not this product.
Can we go straight to the Sprint after this?
Only if the team already has a standard and a process owner. More often the next step is stage 2: turn one person's method into a team standard. A skipped stage comes back as risk in the middle of the sprint.
How many people need to take part?
The product is per seat. A useful group is leaders who decide and who have repeating tasks. Mass staff training does not belong here.

The programme is a fit when

  • → AI use varies significantly across employees
  • → Leaders lack a practical way to assess AI capability
  • → Training has taken place, but no before-and-after evidence exists
  • → Roles need methods tailored to their actual work

The programme is not a fit when

  • ✕ You only need a general introductory seminar
  • ✕ You are not prepared to work on real business tasks
  • ✕ You do not intend to measure the outcome

What we measure
TIME ↓

The same task is completed faster without sacrificing quality.

QUALITY ↑

Outputs are more accurate, consistent and easier to verify.

THROUGHPUT ↑

More valuable work is completed in the same time.

REWORK ↓

Fewer returns, corrections and repeated cycles.

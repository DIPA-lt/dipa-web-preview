Privacy and data protection

We use personal data to reply to enquiries, run the site, and — only with consent — understand how the site is used.

Controller

The data controller is UAB Dipa, company code 305878229, VAT code LT100016633913, Krivių g. 5, LT-01204 Vilnius, Lithuania. Privacy questions: info@dipa.lt or +370 661 10921. Read this together with the cookie policy.

What we process and why

  • Contact form. First and last name, company, work email, phone (if provided), role and the context you describe. Purpose: to reply to and qualify your enquiry. Legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to a contract, and your consent to be contacted.
  • AI maturity assessment. Your answers, the calculated dimension scores, your overall maturity level and — if you provide them — your contact details and preferred contact channel. Purpose: to show your result and, if requested, a specific interpretation. Legal basis: your consent.
  • Necessary cookies. Store your consent choice and support site security. Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating the site.
  • Analytics and marketing cookies. Google Analytics, Facebook and LinkedIn, only after you opt in. We do not enable them without consent.

Who receives the data

Form and assessment data is sent over a secure connection to our internal “DIPA OS” system (running on Google Cloud), where the enquiry is recorded as a lead and qualified. Core contact details may also be synchronised to a DIPA-managed Google Sheet for internal work. We only use processors bound by data-processing agreements, and we do not sell your data.

We never send form text or personal data to analytics events. Your data is not used to train public AI models.

Marketing consent

A separate, unticked consent lets us email you DIPA insights. It is not required to submit an enquiry and can be withdrawn at any time from any email or by contacting us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and assessment data for as long as needed to respond and for reasonable follow-up (normally no more than 24 months after the last contact), then delete or anonymise it. Marketing data is kept until you withdraw consent. Your cookie consent is stored in your browser until you change it or clear cookies.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict, port or object to the processing of your data where we rely on legitimate interest. You can withdraw any consent (including cookies) at any time. You may also complain to the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (vdai.lrv.lt).

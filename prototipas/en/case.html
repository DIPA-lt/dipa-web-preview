01 What it is

DIPA sales SOP + CRM + calls + KPIs in one place

Not another CRM and not a chatbot. The pipeline stays in the system you already run. DIPA OS does the work that used to sit in someone’s head: research, A/B/C/D routing, a playbook before the call, quality after it, and a morning problem layer.

The system does

  • → Deep Research + ICP A/B/C/D in minutes, not 30–60 minutes of manual work
  • → A playbook for this lead: #1 pain, Challenger reframe, next step
  • → Call QA with a DIPA scorecard and a plan for what to fix tomorrow
  • → An ASAICHI advisor: the weakest point and ≤5 actions with a number

The system is not

  • ✕ Your CRM — the pipeline stays where it already is
  • ✕ A chat on every contact
  • ✕ An autodialer or spray outreach
  • ✕ A promise to install what is red — Outside DIPA scope
02 Leads and playbook
Workspace

Intake → Deep Research → A/B/C/D

A contact arrives from a form, the CRM, or is entered by hand. In 2–5 minutes the agent researches, scores maturity and routes. A — ROI stream. B — a good company, but the contact is often a gate opener: the job is to reach the Executive Buyer. C — nurture. D — do not spend the morning.

  • →Company ≠ contactA good company + HR is not a D. Routing says what to do.
  • →Playbook before the call#1 pain, reframe, discovery questions, EB strategy.
  • →A person confirmsThe system does not dial and does not sell a red scope.
03 Call QA and the team
After the call

Every conversation becomes a training rep

You upload the transcript. The system scores it by DIPA meeting type (Discovery, ROI, Custom, Upsell) and eight rubrics: next step, pain lock, Executive Buyer, value articulation, JOLT. It explains why the score is what it is and what to do next time. It does not punish you for what Discovery did not require.

  • →SoftphoneClick-to-dial from the lead card, inbound in the browser, live coach during the call.
  • →TeamCall score, GO rate, most frequent errors by person — a 1:1 with data.
04 ASAICHI

In the morning we see where it burns — and what to change

ASAICHI is the team layer, not the seller’s funnel. Toyota PDCA: anomalies first, then cause, countermeasure, check. The advisor walks result, quality, productivity, flow and efficiency — and returns few actions, always with a number.

KPI BOARD

Day / week / month. Green, amber, red. Focus view — anomalies first.

PROBLEMS · PDCA

Kanban: cause → countermeasure → check. Effect measured as KPI before / after.

ADVISOR

“We took in X €. Why. What we change next week.” 3–5 recommendations. A person confirms — advise-only.

FORECAST · P&L

Are we on the target path. For leadership — not for the daily seller.

The conclusion must end in an action, not in analytics. Navigation: which metric is down → which person → which meeting type → what to change in the process.

05 How the system learns
Agent reviews

You use it. The system gets better.

You do not write prompts. You mark correct / partial / wrong, the error type, and whether the playbook was used. Experts calibrate. A gold set guards against a fix that breaks what already worked.

  • →Feedback on the workNot a chat thread — a record on that lead or QA.
  • →Generate guidelinesErrors become rules for the next cycle.

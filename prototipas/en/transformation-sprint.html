01 Qualification

The sprint works only under certain conditions

We deliberately filter out projects where we cannot prove a result. That protects both sides.

It fits if

  • → The process repeats and can be measured
  • → There is a process owner who can decide
  • → Data exists, or it can be collected
  • → Leadership is ready to change the work, not only the tool
  • → You are looking for a result, not the lowest price

It does not fit if

  • ✕ The problem is unmeasurable, or you disagree on what a result is
  • ✕ Every case is unique — the process does not repeat
  • ✕ There is no owner who can decide
  • ✕ You want only a tool, with no change in the work
  • ✕ There is no data and no plan to have any

04 Method

Before asking “can we automate this?”, we ask “does this need to exist?”

Five filters are applied to every process step in order. Automation is the third choice, not the first.

1ELIMINATEThe widest filter
2SIMPLIFYWithout technology
3AIOnly third
4HUMAN + AIThe usual answer
5HUMANAccountability stays here
1 · ELIMINATE

Is this step needed at all? The largest effect is usually here.

2 · SIMPLIFY

Can the same result be reached more simply, without technology?

3 · AI

Can AI do this fully, at an acceptable error risk?

4 · HUMAN + AI

Does AI prepare, and a person approve? The most common answer.

5 · HUMAN

Is this a decision that must stay with a person — because of responsibility, not ability?

Why the order matters

If you start from the third filter, you automate unnecessary work. The largest value is almost always in the first two filters — and it costs no licences.

Redesign first. Only then automate.

08 Common questions

What leaders ask first

What if after 90 days the result is negative?
The GATE result will be STOP, and we will say so clearly, with numbers. You will have a locked baseline, a process map and the knowledge of why this process did not pay back — that protects you from a much larger expansion investment with no case. Stop is a normal and useful result.
How much time does our team need to give?
The process owner — about 4 hours a week. The team that works the process — 2–3 hours a week in the ADOPT phase. Leadership — one session in FRAME and one in GATE. If that time cannot be given, the sprint will not work, and it is better not to start.
Who owns the system that is built?
You do. Processes, prompts, playbooks, configurations, integration documentation and SOP belong to you. We do not sell licences and take no platform commissions. The aim is that you can work without us after the project.
Do you use our data to train models?
No. Data processing is defined in the contract and the GDPR processing record before the project starts. We choose architecture in which your data does not enter external model training sets.
Why 90 days, not 30 or 180?
Less than 90 days is not enough for ADOPT — behaviour change in live work needs several weeks to show up in measurement, not only in enthusiasm. Longer than 90 days loses sponsorship impulse and the project starts living a life of its own without a decision point.
What is OPPM, and can we buy it separately?
OPPM is the communication overlay we run the sprint on: one page with tasks, objectives, time, cost and owners. It comes with the sprint and stays with you after GATE. We do not sell it as a subscription — without a real project the board is an empty template. See the board →
Do we need to complete stages 1 and 2 first?
Not always. If the team already has a standard, you know the process and you have an owner — you can start at stage 3. If AI is still an individual skill, start at stage 1 or 2. A skipped stage comes back as risk in the middle of the sprint.

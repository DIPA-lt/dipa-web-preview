About DIPA

Our aim is not to make you dependent on DIPA. It is to help your organisation run human–AI work independently.

We help organisations create measurably better work with AI, then transfer the capability to manage, evaluate and improve it without day-to-day reliance on us.

Mission

Measurably better work with AI

To help organisations redesign work between people and AI so the improvement can be evidenced, governed and sustained without DIPA's daily involvement.

Vision

A continuously learning organisation

Organisations continuously reallocate work between people and AI according to where each creates the greatest value.

Technology takes on work that can be delegated reliably. People retain the work where judgement and accountability matter most.

Decisions

Situations requiring context, judgement and a deliberate choice.

Creation

Designing new opportunities, propositions and operating models.

Relationships

Trust, negotiation and genuine human connection.

Accountability

Every final decision and its consequences have a named owner.

Principles we do not compromise

These are operating rules, not abstract values. They guide every project decision.

01

Value before technology

Define the constraint and its economic size before selecting a tool or model.

02

Measure before scaling

Without a baseline and the same metric after the change, there is no proof.

03

Redesign before automating

There is no value in doing obsolete work faster.

04

Capability before dependency

A good engagement strengthens the client rather than locking them into a supplier.

05

Explicit accountability

The more autonomy AI receives, the clearer its limits and the ultimate owner must be.

DIPA Capability System

The entire system must remain under the client's control.

  • Governance
  • KPIs and standards
  • Decision rules
  • AI Work Contract
  • Learning cycle

DIPA may provide periodic assurance, improvement support or expansion expertise, but the process owner always remains on the client side.

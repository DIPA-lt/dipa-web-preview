02 Team

One person's best way of working becomes the whole team's capability

If best practice stays in someone's head, you are still at stage 1. Stage 2 starts when it becomes a skill, playbook, workflow and team standard.

The problem

Individuals learn, but the knowledge stays in their heads. Everyone works their own way. Quality varies. The organisation depends on its strongest people.

What we do

We move best practice from a head into a system: method → skill / playbook / workflow → team standard → KPI and measurement. An owner is appointed to keep the standard alive.

AI Value Diagnostic

Where is your largest AI value gap—and what is the most rational next step?

The diagnostic is not designed to sell an enterprise-wide transformation. It clarifies the current position, sizes the constraint and identifies one near-term opportunity that can be measured.

What will you leave the diagnostic with?

The recommendation may be to buy nothing from DIPA if the best next step is one your team can take independently.

01

Current position

Where AI is already in use and what has materially changed.

02

Primary value gap

Individual performance, team capability, process design or business model.

03

Economic size

What the constraint costs and what scale of intervention is justified.

04

Rational next step

One action—not a long, abstract transformation roadmap.

05

Primary KPI

The metric that will allow the change to be assessed objectively.

06

Recommended route

A productivity programme, Transformation Sprint or internal action.

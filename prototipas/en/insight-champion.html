Enabling the champion: from coordinator to portfolio owner

If after the project the organisation still cannot take a GATE decision itself, you bought dependency, not capability.

Stage 2 moves best practice from one person's head into a team standard. That is not enough for scale. Scale needs a person who can filter processes, judge value and risk, and say Stop.

That is why DIPA stages 3 and 4 deliberately develop an internal champion. Not because it sounds good. Because without them every new unit starts as if it were the first.

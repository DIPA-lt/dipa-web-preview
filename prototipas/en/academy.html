02 Method

Why we teach with your work, not examples

A course built on invented exercises ends in enthusiasm. Two weeks later the team is back to old habits.

1 · TASK

Each participant brings a real repeating job from their own week.

2 · METHOD

Context, boundaries, verification — not a prompt list to copy.

3 · PRACTICE

The work is done during the session, not left as homework.

4 · CAPTURE

The working method is written down so it can be repeated and handed over.

Baseline AI literacy is a module, not a product. It enables transformation but does not replace it.
